# Dog Costume Contest
## A warm, simple guide for the whole team

Welcome. This little app is here so event day feels easy — especially for the MC, family helpers, and anyone holding a tablet in the middle of a happy, noisy crowd.

**This website is only the costume contest.**  
Tickets, parking, and event admission stay on **montclair-dog.com**. Point people there if they ask about getting in.

Think of the contest in two rooms:

- **The crowd** (phones) — sign up, cheer, vote, celebrate  
- **The stage team** (tablet or laptop) — introduce dogs, open rounds, pick finalists, name winners  

Guests never see vote counts. That stays with you, so the reveal still feels like a show.

---

# 1. The idea in one page

Dogs arrive, get a number, and go on stage in **small groups of 10**. That is a **round**.

While a round is live, the crowd picks **one favorite** from those ten. Then you do the next round. Nobody has to vote on 30 dogs at once. The MC can tell each dog’s story. The crowd stays with you.

After every dog has been introduced, you look at the rankings and **choose finalists**. Those dogs go on to the **five prizes**. The crowd votes once in each award. You look at the ranking, then **you name the five winners**. The crowd’s numbers help you. They do not trap you.

When all five winners are chosen, a **congratulations** page appears for everyone — photos, names, costumes, the whole sweet ending.

That is the whole show.

---

# 2. Who does what

You do not all need to do everything. Split it however feels natural.

### The MC
Lives on **MC script**. Reads each dog’s story. Looks at the photo. Says the name, the costume, the fun fact, and who they’re with. You are the heart of the contest.

### A round helper (husband, sister, or staff)
Lives on **Rounds** and **Dashboard**. Opens one round when those dogs walk out. Closes it when the group is done. Later, starts prize voting.

### A signup helper
Lives on **Signup** and **Dogs**. Helps walk-ins register. Checks that names look right. Optional photos are lovely for the MC, but not required.

### The crowd
Uses **Signup**, **Contestants**, **Vote**, and **Winners** on their own phones. One vote per phone, per round, and one vote per prize.

If you are short-handed, one calm person with a tablet can run Rounds + Dashboard, and the MC can stay on MC script. That is enough.

# 3. How to log in (admins only)

1. Open the contest website  
2. Go to **/admin** (or tap your bookmark)  
3. Sign in with the admin email you were given  
4. You should land on **Dashboard**

You will see buttons along the top:

**Dashboard · MC script · Dogs · Rounds · Finalists · Results**

That is your backstage.

If login says you are not allowed, you are probably on a guest Gmail. Use the event admin email. A private / incognito window often helps if an old login is stuck.

# 4. Guest pages (what people on phones see)

These are the four words in the top menu.

### Signup
Walk-ins and anyone who hasn’t registered yet. They enter:

- Dog’s name  
- Handler’s name and contact  
- Costume  
- Breed, inspiration, and a fun fact (these become the MC’s lines)  
- An optional photo  

Then they get a number like **DOG-007** and a thank-you screen. Keep that number. Dogs are grouped automatically, first come first served, **10 per round**.

If a photo fails, keep going. The contest still works. The MC can still introduce them.

### Contestants
This is **round voting**.  
When you open Round 2, guests see Round 2’s dogs and pick **one favorite**. One vote per phone for that round. When the round is over, they wait for the next one.

If this page looks empty, a round is not open yet. That is normal. Open one on **Rounds**.

### Vote
This is **prize voting**, and it stays closed until you are ready.  
After finalists are saved and you tap **Start prize voting**, guests vote once in each of the five awards. They only see finalists — not the whole lineup. That keeps it fair and fast.

### Winners
Before you name all five winners, this page simply waits.  
After you choose all five, it becomes the celebration: each prize, each dog, photo and story. Put it on a screen if you can. People love seeing it.

# 5. The five prizes (say these out loud)

1. **Fur-right Night Award** — Spookiest Costume  
2. **The Best Furiends Award** — Best Duo or Group  
3. **Paws-itively Hilarious** — Most Hilarious Costume  
4. **Pup Culture Award** — Best TV, Film, Music, or Celebrity Costume  
5. **Best in Show** — Overall Winner  

People may vote in all five. One vote per prize, per phone.

---

# 6. Admin pages (what you see backstage)

### Dashboard
Your home base. How many dogs, which round is open, whether prize voting is live.  
**Start prize voting** lives here. Do not tap it until finalists are saved.

### MC script
Cue cards, already filled in from signup. Grouped by round, in order.

For each dog:

- Number (DOG-012) and place in the round  
- Photo  
- Name  
- Breed  
- “Dressed as…”  
- Inspiration  
- Fun fact  
- Handler’s name  

Read it like a story, not a list. A photo plus a fun fact is usually enough magic.

### Dogs
The full roster. Edit a spelling, a costume line, or a fact if someone runs up and says “we changed outfits.” You can also mark a finalist here, but **Finalists** is the easier place to do that with vote counts in front of you.

### Rounds
The stage manager’s page.

For each round you can:

- **Open round** — this group is on stage; crowd can vote on Contestants  
- **Close** — voting for that group stops  
- **Complete** — you are done with that group  

**Open only one round at a time.** If you open Round 3, Round 2 is no longer the live one.

Below the buttons, rankings show 1st / 2nd / 3rd by introduction votes. After you save finalists, those lists settle on the finalists so prize time stays clear.

### Finalists
This is the pause between “we met everyone” and “now the prizes.”

Every round is listed with vote counts, highest first. Check who should go through. Trust the crowd, and also trust the room — a quiet costume that made people gasp still belongs.

Tap **Save finalists**.  
From that moment, **Vote** and **Results** show **only those dogs**.

### Results
Prize time, for your eyes only.

Each of the five awards has a ranked list of finalists. Big number = crowd votes.  
Tap **Choose winner** on the dog you want for that prize. You may follow 1st place. You may not. It is your contest.

When the fifth winner is chosen, prize voting closes on its own, and guests get the congratulations page. That is the ribbon on the day.

# 7. Suggested event-day flow

You can loosen this. It is a rhythm, not a script of the whole festival.

### Before the first dog
- Admin is signed in on a tablet (Rounds + Dashboard)  
- MC is signed in on a second tablet or the same one, on **MC script**  
- Signup is open on a phone or a sign with a QR code  
- Tell the crowd: “When a round starts, open Contestants and pick your favorite of that group.”

### During each round
1. Helper taps **Open round**  
2. MC introduces each dog from **MC script**  
3. Crowd votes on **Contestants**  
4. Give them a moment after the last dog  
5. Helper closes or completes the round  
6. Breathe. Open the next round when the next group is ready  

### After the last round
1. Look at **Finalists** (or the rankings on Rounds)  
2. Check the dogs going through  
3. **Save finalists**  
4. On Dashboard, **Start prize voting**  
5. Tell the crowd: “Now vote for the five prizes. You’ll only see the finalists.”  
6. Give them time. Watch the vote number on Dashboard if you like  

### Naming winners
1. Open **Results**  
2. For each prize, glance at 1st / 2nd / 3rd  
3. Choose the winner  
4. When all five are set, open **Winners** for the room  
5. MC announces. People clap. Dogs lose their hats. Perfect.

# 8. Two kinds of voting (this is the part people mix up)

| | Introduction votes | Prize votes |
|---|-------------------|-------------|
| Where guests go | **Contestants** | **Vote** |
| When | While a round is open | After you start prize voting |
| Who they see | Dogs in that round of 10 | Finalists only |
| What they’re picking | “My favorite in this group” | One dog per prize |
| Who sees the counts | Staff only | Staff only |

Introduction votes help you pick **finalists**.  
Prize votes help you pick **winners**.  
You still tap the names.

# 9. Words you can say to the crowd

**At signup**  
“Grab your phone, tap Signup, and you’ll get a number. Walk-ins are welcome.”

**Before Round 1**  
“We’re bringing dogs out in groups of ten. When your group is up, open Contestants and pick one favorite.”

**Between rounds**  
“That’s Round 2 wrapped. Hang tight — Round 3 is next.”

**Before prizes**  
“We’ve picked our finalists. Open Vote and go through the five awards. You can vote in all of them.”

**At the end**  
“Winners are up on the Winners page — go look at those beautiful dogs.”

---

# 10. Kind rules (print this in your head)

- Walk-ins can sign up all day.  
- One live round at a time.  
- One favorite per phone, per round.  
- One vote per prize, per phone.  
- Photos are a gift, not a requirement.  
- Rankings are a flashlight, not a handcuff.  
- Guests never see the scores.  
- Tickets are not in this app.  
- If a moment is messy, pause the round. The app will wait.

# 11. If something feels stuck

**“I can’t get into admin.”**  
Use the admin email. Try a private window. Don’t use a random personal Gmail unless it was set up as admin.

**“Contestants is empty.”**  
No round is open. Open one on **Rounds**.

**“Vote says it isn’t open yet.”**  
Save finalists first, then **Start prize voting** on the Dashboard.

**“Results has no dogs.”**  
Finalists haven’t been saved, or they were cleared. Go to **Finalists**, check dogs, save again.

**“Winners still says it’s waiting.”**  
A winner is still missing in one of the five prizes. Finish **Results**.

**“Someone’s name is spelled wrong.”**  
**Dogs** → find them → edit → save. The MC script updates.

**“They didn’t get a photo.”**  
Totally fine. Introduce them anyway.

**“We opened the wrong round.”**  
Open the right one. Only one stays live.

**“Can we change a winner?”**  
Yes. On Results you can unset and choose again. The congratulations page follows whatever is saved.

# 12. A tiny checklist for whoever holds the tablet

Before  
- [ ] Admin signed in  
- [ ] MC script loads and shows dogs  
- [ ] Signup works on a phone  

During  
- [ ] One round open at a time  
- [ ] Crowd pointed to Contestants  
- [ ] Round closed before the next group  

After introductions  
- [ ] Finalists saved  
- [ ] Prize voting started  
- [ ] Crowd pointed to Vote  

Ending  
- [ ] Five winners chosen  
- [ ] Winners page shown to the room  

---

# 13. Why it was built this way

A costume contest should feel like a party, not a spreadsheet.

Rounds keep the stage human. Stories stay short. Voting stays fair. The MC gets real lines instead of scribbled cards. Family helpers get big buttons instead of a manual. And at the end, the whole crowd gets a page of winners they can screenshot and send to grandma.

You still run the show. The app just holds the clipboard.
